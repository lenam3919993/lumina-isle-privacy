1. Privacy Policy (English)

Lumina Isle: Eco Beam Puzzle (package com.lumina.isle) is a family-friendly (PEGI 3 / USK 0) optical puzzle and 3D island sanctuary game. We run no private analytics or tracking servers; network connectivity is used strictly by certified Google Play / Google Mobile Ads SDKs.

1.1 Google AdMob & EU UMP Consent (GDPR Art. 6 & TCF v2.2)

  • Advertising SDK: Lumina Isle uses Google AdMob to display Banner, Interstitial, Rewarded Video, and App Open ads. In the EEA, UK, and Switzerland, the app presents Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form before loading ads.
  • Data Processed by Google: Depending on your consent choice, Google AdMob may process your Device / Advertising ID (AD_ID), approximate IP-based location, ad interaction metrics, and crash/diagnostic signals.
  • Withdraw Consent Anytime: Open Home → Legal Center (&shield;) → Manage Ad Privacy Consent inside the app at any time to change or withdraw consent. Purchasing Remove Ads or VIP Monthly Pass disables all forced banner, interstitial, and app-open ads.

1.2 Google Play Billing (Optional In-App Purchases & Subscriptions)

  • All 1,000 puzzle stages can be played 100% free. Optional Diamond packs, bundles (Starter Pack, Remove Ads, Citadel Architect), and the auto-renewing VIP Monthly Pass are processed exclusively by Google Play Billing.
  • We never see or store your payment card details. Only non-sensitive purchase entitlement tokens and unlock states are stored locally on your device. Subscriptions renew monthly unless cancelled via Google Play Subscriptions.

1.3 Local Game Progress Storage & Your GDPR Rights (Art. 15, 17, 20)

  • Stage stars, Essence balance, unlocked islands, creature evolutions, and settings remain stored locally in Android SharedPreferences (lumina_isle_v3_prefs).
  • Right of Access & Portability (Art. 15 & 20): Export a human-readable + JSON summary via Home → GDPR & Data → View Local Data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Permanently erase all local save data via Home → GDPR & Data → Delete All Local Data, or uninstall the app.

1.4 EU DSA Odds Transparency

  • Lucky Wheel: Equal 1/6 (16.67%) chance per slice.
  • Mystery Chest: 100% Essence (12–30) + 35% Hint (+1) + 8.0% Unowned Creature roll.
  • Wild Creature Encounter: Easy 1.5%, Medium 2.5%, Hard/Expert 4.5%, Master/Hell 7.5% (+4% per Eco Magnetism upgrade).
