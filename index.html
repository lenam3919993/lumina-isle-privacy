1. Privacy Policy (English)

Lumina Isle: Eco Beam Puzzle (package com.lumina.isle) is an independent, 100% offline optical puzzle and 3D island sanctuary game designed with Privacy by Design and by Default (EU GDPR Art. 5 & Art. 25).

1.1 Zero Personal Data Collection

  • No Data Collected or Shared: The app does not collect, store remotely, process, sell, or share any personal data, device identifiers, Advertising IDs (AD_ID), location data, contacts, or analytics.
  • Zero Network Permissions: The app declares zero Android permissions (no INTERNET permission) and includes no third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

1.2 Strictly Local Game Progress Storage (ePrivacy / TDDDG § 25)

Your gameplay progress (unlocked stages, star ratings, virtual Essence balance, unlocked islands, creature collection, evolution levels, language choice, and quest state) is stored exclusively inside your device's private sandbox (SharedPreferences). Cloud auto-backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false").

1.3 Your EU GDPR Rights (Art. 15, 17, 20)

  • Right of Access & Portability (Art. 15 & 20): View and export a full human-readable and JSON summary of your local save data anytime inside the app via Home → GDPR & Data → View Local Data.
  • Right to Erasure (Art. 17): Permanently delete 100% of your local save data with one confirmed tap via Home → GDPR & Data → Delete All Local Data, or by uninstalling the app.

1.4 EU DSA Virtual Currency & Probability Transparency

Lumina Isle contains zero real-money purchases (0€), zero advertisements, and zero real-money gambling. All virtual rewards are earned purely through gameplay with transparent mathematical odds:

  • Lucky Wheel: Equal 1/6 (16.67%) chance per slice (12 Essence, 1 Hint, 25 Essence, 1 Mystery Chest, 75 Essence, or Unowned Creature / +55 Essence fallback).
  • Mystery Chest: 100% Essence (12–30) + 100% Hint (+1) + 8.0% Unowned Creature roll.
  • Wild Creature Encounter per Stage: Easy 1.5%, Medium 2.5%, Hard/Expert 4.5%, Master/Hell 7.5% (+3% per Eco Magnetism upgrade level).
